WHY CENTROID MISMATCHES OCCUR
------------------------------

The small centroid differences (1-3mm) between original aligned atlases and the
final combined Levtiades atlas are expected and occur due to:

1. ATLAS PROCESSING EFFECTS:
   • Original aligned atlas: Each region exists as pure, isolated ROI
   • Final combined atlas: Regions undergo multiple processing steps:
     - Combination with other atlases (Levinson + Tian + Destrieux)
     - Overlap resolution (hierarchical priority: Midbrain > Subcortical > Cortical)
     - Sequential reindexing (1-207)
     - Hemisphere reordering (LEFT before RIGHT)

2. VOXEL-LEVEL CHANGES:
   • Boundary voxel loss/gain during overlap resolution
   • Small shifts from interpolation during processing
   • Edge effects from multiple transformation steps

3. WHY SOME REGIONS MATCH PERFECTLY (0.0mm):
   • No overlapping voxels with other atlases
   • Not affected by boundary adjustments
   • Maintained exact original shape through all processing

4. CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
   • 1-3mm differences are clinically acceptable for brain atlases at 2mm resolution
   • Small shifts don't affect functional integrity of regions
   • Regions maintain anatomical identity and boundaries
   • Multi-atlas combination always introduces small centroid shifts
